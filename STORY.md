## The premise

**Melange Mercantile is a melange trading company, not a House.** It is licensed by CHOAM (the
Imperium's great trading combine, which controls the melange trade in the books) and known for its
discretion. Its clients are the Great Houses. The site is how a House arranges a private supply of
melange alongside the tightly controlled official one.

The story the site tells: **from the deep desert to your hall.** Every chapter answers one question
a House's buyer would ask, in the order they would ask it, building toward the letter.

## House style (applies everywhere)

**Naming**
- The company is always **Melange Mercantile**, in full. Never "the House", "the Mercantile" or "we"
  in headings. "We" and "our" are fine in running sentences.
- **"House" belongs only to clients**: "your House", "the Great Houses".
- **Always "melange", never "spice".** It's the proper name, and Melange Mercantile is too refined to
  use the common word. The one exception is the logo's tagline, *Spice · Trade · Influence*,
  which stays as it is.

**Vocabulary.** Melange Mercantile talks like a merchant, not a noble.

| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| charter, chartered | (claims of rank or rule) |
| ledger | register (company records) |
| factor (the company's agent) | steward |
| assay, assayed | tested |
| consignment | order, shipment |
| allocation, request | buy, order, cart, shop |

**Two words that must not overlap**
- **Warrant:** a client's standing with Melange Mercantile. Long-trusted Houses hold one. It is what
  unlocks Imperial Reserve.
- **Guarantee:** a consignment's assurance of condition, which runs for a set time after sealing and
  can lapse. *Today the site uses "warrant" for this; it changes to "guarantee" in Verify.*

**Lore guardrails**
- Only the **Spacing Guild** travels between worlds. Melange Mercantile never "flies" a consignment
  off Arrakis. Its frigates lift cargo *up to* a Guild heighliner.
- Dates are written **10,181 AG** ("After Guild", as in the books), not "10.181 AG", which reads as
  a decimal.
- Melange is found on **one world only**: Arrakis.

**Grades and basins**

| Grade | Purity | Basin | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperial Reserve | 99.4% | **Imperial Basin** *(was "Arrakis Basin")* | By warrant |
| House Select | 97.1% | Sihaya Ridge | Available |
| Guild Standard | 94.8% | Hagga Basin *(was "Hagga Rift")* | By volume |
| Raw Harvest | 88.2% | Cielago Deep | By mass |

- "Arrakis Basin" doesn't work as one of four basins, because all four are on Arrakis. **Imperial
  Basin** is a real place in the books and pairs naturally with Imperial Reserve. **Decided:** it
  stays, as part of the cheek.
- **Hagga Basin** is the name in the books. "Rift" was invented, so it may as well match.
- "Sihaya" is a real Fremen word ("desert spring"), and **Cielago** is a real place. Both are fine
  as they are.

**Lore note (after the first render):** melange forms beneath the desert and is harvested as
spice-rich sand, then separated and refined. The four grades follow that: Raw Harvest is still in
its sand, Imperial Reserve is the purest. The first render got the composition and emblems right
but made grades 3 and 4 dark brown like coffee and soil, and gave the vessels kitchen spice-shaker
lids. The fix, sent as an edit to that render: domed pewter stoppers sealed with a band of black
wax, and contents running from glowing pure melange to ochre dune sand flecked with rust-orange.

*If the first results look too much like a laboratory, swap "smoked glass vessels" for "small
pewter urns with smoked glass windows". If the four powders look too alike, add "the difference
in refinement must be clearly visible". If the emblem comes out warped (most likely on the small
tags), follow up with "keep the emblem only on the caps, larger, and match the attached shape
exactly". When you have one you like, I'll grade it with the same
treatment as the other photos (desaturate, crush blacks, cool shadows, warm highlights).*

**Why:** this is the missing chapter, the actual offer. The cheek works because it explains
something odd (how a company sells an "Imperial" grade) with a wink rather than ignoring it.
"Warrants are extended, never applied for" tells you exclusivity without saying "exclusive".
